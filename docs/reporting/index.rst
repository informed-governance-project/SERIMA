Reporting
=========

Purpose
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**The Reporting Module is intended exclusively for regulators. Only users with the roles of Regulator Admin or Regulator User can access and view the Reporting Module.**

The module enables regulators to generate and distribute annual reports to operators. Based on predefined criteria, the regulator can create reports that analyze cybersecurity performance over time. For example, if 2026 is selected as the base year and compared against data from the previous three years, operators can gain insight into how their cybersecurity posture has evolved, identifying areas of improvement or decline.

By generating reports, regulators can view the operators’ positions within different sectors, assess whether they are developing appropriately, and identify areas where further improvement is possible.

**Access to the Reporting Module is granted by the Platform Admin. The corresponding permission can be enabled for each regulator through the Functionalities table in the Platform Admin's back-office interface.**

The homepage
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

After logging in as a Regulator Admin or Regulator User, the Homepage is displayed with three main tiles in the center of the screen.
To access the Reporting Module, click the **Go to dashboard** button on the Reporting tile. This will take you to the Reporting Dashboard, where you can view and manage reporting-related information and activities.

.. figure:: /_static/images/reporting/overview-01.png
   :alt: Reporting
   :target: ../_static/images/reporting/overview-01.png

Once you are on the Dashboard, you can switch between modules by clicking the **module selector** drop-down menu in the top-left corner, next to the **Home** button (which displays the SERIMA logo).
