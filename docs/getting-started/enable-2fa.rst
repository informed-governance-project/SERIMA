Two-factor authentication
-------------------------

Two-factor authentication (2FA) protects your account with a second step after your password:
a six-digit token generated by an authenticator app on your smartphone, or by a password manager.

Enable two-factor authentication
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The first time you log in (see :doc:`login`), the platform suggests you enable two-factor authentication.
Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-01.png
   :alt: Account security page with the Enable Two-Factor Authentication button
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-01.png

A wizard guides you through the set-up. Click **Next** to start it.

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-02.png
   :alt: First step of the two-factor authentication wizard
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-02.png

Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or type the **TOTP Secret** shown below it into your
authenticator or password manager to set up **TOTP** (Time-based One-Time Password) manually.
Then enter the six-digit token it shows into the **Token** field and click **Next**.

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-03.png
   :alt: Two-factor authentication wizard with the QR code and the token field
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-03.png

Once the token is accepted, two-factor authentication is enabled:

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-04.png
   :alt: Confirmation that two-factor authentication has been set
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-04.png

Click **Two-Factor Authentication has been set. Please log in again.** and log in with your new second step, as described below.

Log in with a token
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

From now on, every login asks for a token after your email address and password.
Open your authenticator app, type the six-digit token it currently shows for SERIMA, and click **Login**.

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-05.png
   :alt: Login step asking for the two-factor authentication token
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-05.png

You then land on the homepage. Its parts are described in :doc:`homepage`.

.. figure:: /_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-06.png
   :alt: Homepage of an Operator User after logging in with a token
   :target: ../_static/images/getting-started/enable-2fa-06.png

Backup tokens
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Backup tokens are one-time codes you can use instead of the token from your authenticator,
for example when your smartphone is not at hand. Generate them from **Security** in the account menu
(see :doc:`homepage`), and keep them somewhere safe: each one works only once.
At login, click **Use backup token** on the token step and enter one of them.

Lost your smartphone?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Log in with a backup token, then set up two-factor authentication again on your new device from **Security**.
If you have no backup token left, ask an administrator of your organisation to reset your two-factor authentication.
At your next login, the platform guides you through enabling it again, as described above.
