Operator admin
--------------

An operator administrator manages the accounts of their operator. Besides the administration console, they use
the incident notification and security objectives modules for their operator (see :doc:`/getting-started/roles-and-permissions`).
In the console, they create the operator administrators and users of their operator, approve or reject the incident
user accounts that a regulator user suggests as members of it, and keep the email address and phone number
of the operator up to date.
The operator itself, its sectors and its first administrator are set up by the regulator
(see :ref:`regulator-user-operators`).

Open the console with **Settings** in the header of the platform (see :doc:`/administration/index`).

.. figure:: /_static/images/administration/operator-admin/index-01.png
   :alt: Site administration page of an operator administrator with the Governance section, the Recent actions panel and the Return to user interface link outlined
   :target: ../../_static/images/administration/operator-admin/index-01.png

The console has one section and a panel:

- **Governance**: your operator in **Operators** and its accounts in **Users**, see :doc:`governance`.
- **Recent actions**: your own latest changes in the console.

**Operators** shows only **Change**: you cannot create an operator.

.. note::

   When your account belongs to several operators, the console works on the operator you chose at login,
   and **Settings** is shown only when you are an administrator of that operator.
   To manage another operator, log out and log in again (see :doc:`/getting-started/login`).

.. tip::

   To give a colleague access to the platform, create their account in **Users** (see :ref:`operator-admin-users`).
   They then choose a password and set up two-factor authentication themselves.
